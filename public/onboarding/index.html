Webetu account setup

Save your Webetu credentials in Passbolt

Open Passbolt

Checklist

  1. Sign in to Passbolt.
  2. Open the shared Webetu resource.
  3. Edit the item.
  4. Replace the placeholder username and password.
  5. Save the resource.

Do not rename the resource. Only change the Username and Password fields.

1

Sign in

Open Passbolt and sign in with your account email and passphrase.

2

Find the shared item

On the Home page, look for the resource whose name starts with webetu/users/.

3

Select it and click Edit

Select the Webetu resource, then click Edit in the toolbar.

4

Replace the placeholders

Replace the placeholder Username with your Webetu username. Replace the placeholder Password with your Webetu password.

5

Save

Click Save. After saving, the worker can use the credentials without anyone else seeing your password.

6

Sign out on shared devices

If you are using a shared computer, open the account menu and sign out when you finish.
